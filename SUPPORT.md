# Support

The Kit is provided as is, with no warranty (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

- **Best effort only.** Questions and bug reports are welcome through GitHub issues on this repository for people who have access. Answers are best effort and may not come at all.
- **No service level.** There is no response time, resolution time, uptime, or on-call commitment, and no guarantee of fixes or future updates.
- **Not covered:** your warehouse administration, cloud costs, credentials, custom modelling, client-specific sources, or heavily modified projects.
- **Never post secrets or data.** Do not include credentials, `profiles.yml`, `.env` files, or customer data in an issue. Report suspected security problems privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public issue.

Versioning and which release line gets fixes are set out in decision 0002.

Paid support, response targets, and update entitlements are not offered yet. If they are, they will be set out in a separate written agreement. The reasoning is recorded in decision 0001.
